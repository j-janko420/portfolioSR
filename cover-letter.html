Studio Sera Interior Designer / Exhibition Specialist
Application Letter For consideration

Dear Hiring Manager,

I am writing to express my interest in the interior design opportunity within your studio. With a background in luxury interiors, exhibition design and event-led spatial storytelling, I bring a creative approach shaped by detail, atmosphere and purposeful functionality.

Over the past several years, I have contributed to residential, hospitality and exhibition projects where the objective was to create experiences that feel elevated, memorable and deeply connected to the people who inhabit them. I am particularly drawn to studios that value narrative design, strong material palettes and the ability to merge cultural sensitivity with refined craftsmanship.

My work continually balances visual elegance with practical planning, from concept development and mood boards to material specification, lighting composition and final styling. I enjoy collaborating across teams, understanding client aspirations and translating them into spaces that feel both immersive and enduring.

I would welcome the opportunity to bring my design perspective, professionalism and passion for meaningful interiors to your team. Thank you for your time and consideration. I look forward to the possibility of discussing how I may contribute to your projects and design vision.

Sincerely,
Studio Sera
